# Application Packet Template

Use this structure when manually refining generated packets:

## Candidate

Name, location, email, website, LinkedIn.

## Role Focus

Role track and why the JD maps to that track.

## Tailored Summary

Two to four lines that connect Ao's strongest verified evidence to the JD.

## Selected Evidence

Three to five projects or experiences. Each should include:

- Why it matches the JD.
- Two or three factual bullets.
- Metrics only when present in the source context.

## Tailored Resume Bullets

Use action, scope, method, and outcome. Do not invent tools or metrics.

## Application Answers

Draft concise answers for:

- Why this role.
- Strongest relevant project.
- Technical challenge.
- Working with ambiguity or customer workflows.

## Human Approval Gate

Do not submit until Ao approves the exact final packet.
